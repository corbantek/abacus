Abacus operates Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:30 PM. We're closed on major federal holidays and for a brief winter break between Christmas and New Year's.
Yes. We offer both full-time (5 days) and part-time (3-day and 2-day) schedules for most age groups, subject to availability. Contact us to check current openings for your child's age group.
All Abacus lead teachers hold degrees in Early Childhood Education or a related field. They complete a rigorous onboarding program and receive ongoing professional development throughout the year. Our low turnover means your child builds long-lasting bonds with their teachers.
We maintain some of the lowest ratios in the Austin area: Infants 4:1, Toddlers 5:1, Preschool 8:1, Pre-K 9:1, and Kindergarten 10:1. This ensures every child receives personalized attention throughout the day.
